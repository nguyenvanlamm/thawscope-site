How to play

Each level hides one English word, frozen at the centre of the scope. Type any word to place a probe: the closer its meaning, the hotter it reads (0° to 100°) and the nearer the centre it lands, and words related to each other land in the same direction. Warm probes thaw the ice: first the letter count (Crack), then the first letter (Thin ice), the last letter (Slush) and most of the word (Meltwater). Name the word before your probes run out. A word the game does not know, or one you already tried, costs nothing.

Tools: a Flare places a free probe about twice as close as your best one, a Pick chips out the next letter, a Battery adds five probes. You earn tools by clearing levels 4, 8 and 12 of each set for the first time and by solving the Daily Core.

Purchases

All 120 levels and the Daily Core are free. The Flare Bundle and the Supply Drop add tools; the Field Journal opens every past Daily Core beyond the last seven days. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases. The Field Journal is restored; tools already delivered stay on the device that received them.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
