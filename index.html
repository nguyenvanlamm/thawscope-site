How it plays

Distance tells you how close you are. Direction tells you where to turn. A run of cold guesses still points somewhere.

  1. Type a word

    Any English word. It lands on the scope as a probe; an unknown or repeated word costs nothing.

  2. Read the scope

    Hotter probes sit nearer the centre, from 0° to 100°. Words close to each other in meaning cluster on one side.

  3. Thaw the core

    Warm probes crack the ice and give up letters. Name the frozen word before your probes run out and the core refreezes.

  • Crack: the letter count
  • Thin ice: the first letter
  • Slush: the last letter
  • Meltwater: most of the word

A path longer than one puzzle a day

Ten themed Core sets to work through, and a daily word for the mornings.

10 Core sets, 120 levels

Music, Pantry, Weather, Feelings, Body, Sport, Ocean, City, Garden and Workshop, 12 levels each, up to three stars per level. Every level is free.

Daily Core

One word a day with unlimited probes and a streak. The last seven days stay open to replay.

A limited supply of probes

Each level gives 20 to 30 probes, so every guess counts. A retry brings a different frozen word, not the one you just saw.

Three tools for when you are stuck

A Flare drops a free probe about twice as close as your best. A Pick chips out the next letter. A Battery adds five probes.

Questions

Is Thawscope free?

Yes. All 120 levels and the Daily Core are free, with no ads. Optional purchases: the Flare Bundle (6 Flares), the Supply Drop (10 Flares, 8 Picks and 6 Batteries) and the Field Journal, which opens every past Daily Core beyond the last seven days.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The word data ships with the game, so it plays fully offline. Only purchases go through the App Store.

Which words count?

English dictionary words. Plurals and other forms count as their base word, so "apples" is read as "apple". A word the game does not know, or one you already tried, costs no probe.

Does the game collect any data?

No. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
