Privacy Policy

Thawscope · last updated November 16, 2026

Thawscope is a word puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the stars of each level, how many times you tried each level, which Daily Cores you solved, on which days, and how many probes they took, the day of your last Daily reward, and whether you have seen the first-level guide.
  • Your Flares, Picks and Batteries.
  • Your settings: Sound on or off, Haptics on or off.
  • Whether you have bought the Field Journal, and the store's transaction identifiers of purchases already delivered, so a purchase is never granted twice.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No network requests of its own: the word data ships inside the app.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

All 120 levels and the Daily Core are free. Purchases (Flare Bundle, Supply Drop, Field Journal) are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
